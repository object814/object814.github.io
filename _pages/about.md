I am a first-year DPhil student at the [Applied Artificial Intelligence Lab](https://ori.ox.ac.uk/labs/a2i), University of Oxford. My research is in robot learning for general-purpose manipulation. I focus on world models that keep learning over time in a modular, compositional way.

Before Oxford, I earned an MSc in Robotics from the National University of Singapore, working with [Professor Lin Shao](https://linsats.github.io/), and a BEng in Robotics Engineering from [Beihang University](https://ev.buaa.edu.cn/). Feel free to get in touch about any of my projects.
